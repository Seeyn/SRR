

### Beyond Risky Activities: Bridging the Supervision Gap for Situational Risk Reasoning

**NeurIPS 2026 · Evaluations and Datasets Track**

[**🤗 Dataset (Hugging Face)**](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Anonymous-SRR/SRR) 

> **An activity can be safe in isolation but unsafe in context.** SRR helps vision-language models (VLMs) recognize risks that emerge from the interaction between a user's intended activity and the surrounding visual scenario.

## Responsible Use and Limitations

SRR is intended for **research on VLM post-training and situational safety reasoning**. Its synthesized images and automatically generated reasoning targets may contain imperfections; the human validation results are based on a sample, not an exhaustive audit of every training example. Performance on the reported benchmarks does not establish that a model is reliable enough for safety-critical real-world deployment.
